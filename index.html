How do I get my export?
  1. In Instagram, open Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions.
  2. Tap Download your information and choose your Instagram account.
  3. Pick Some of your information → Content (posts, stories, reels), format JSON, date range Last year.
  4. When Instagram emails you, download the .zip and drop it here.

We only open the files that describe your own posts. Messages, followers and everything else in the export are ignored.
